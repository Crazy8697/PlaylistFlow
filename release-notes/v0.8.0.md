Test build of Playlist Flow for Windows, on the way to version 1.0.0. If you have 0.7.0, it
offers this update by itself (or Help > Check for updates).

What's new:

- A new icon: tracks flowing into one, on a faint music staff. It shows on the program, its
  windows, the taskbar and the installer.
- Your FreqBlog allowance goes much further. Playlists go through FreqBlog's bulk lookup, 50
  tracks a call; tracks already looked up are never asked about again; tracks FreqBlog is still
  analysing are checked again a few minutes later instead of being asked about over and over;
  and tracks it doesn't know are asked about again after a week.
- FreqBlog usage readout: setup and Settings show "X of Y requests used this month, resets
  <date>", in a warning colour from 90%. It comes from FreqBlog's free key check, which doesn't
  count against your allowance, and nothing polls it.
- A real FreqBlog key check in setup and in Settings' Test button. If FreqBlog turns a key
  down, you see FreqBlog's own explanation.
- Signing in to Spotify now finishes by itself: approve in your browser and the browser says
  you can close the tab. Playlist Flow listens on 127.0.0.1 (this PC only) just for that moment.
  Pasting the address still works if the port can't be used.
- Pasting a key with extra text around it (a label, spaces, line breaks) now works, and a key
  that still doesn't fit says why.
- Fixes: editing a BPM in felt view stored the wrong value; long playlists named only their
  first 50 tracks in confirmations; a removed track in a playlist could stop it loading; damaged
  playlist files, unreadable replies and dropped connections now give a plain message instead of
  an error; reordering is paused while BPM/key lookups run; the sign-in checks Spotify's state
  value.
- The privacy policy now covers the sign-in listener and the key check (version 2026-10-17.2).
  Playlist Flow asks you to accept the new version the next time you sign in to Spotify.

BPM and key values are estimates. You need Spotify Premium, your own free Spotify developer app
and your own free FreqBlog key; setup walks you through it.
