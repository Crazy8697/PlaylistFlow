Test build of Playlist Flow for Windows: a free program that orders your Spotify playlists so
they flow, by musical key and tempo. Works with Spotify.

This is a test build on the way to version 1.0.0. Install it with PlaylistFlow-Setup.exe; from
here on it updates itself to each new build (Help > Check for updates, or the update bar).

What's in it:

- The DarkRelay look: grouped toolbars, boxed side panel, and a new About box.
- A setup wizard: your own Spotify developer app, sign-in, your FreqBlog key, an optional
  GetSongBPM key, and where to keep your playlists.
- The privacy policy and end user agreement, shown in setup before you sign in, and Settings >
  Disconnect Spotify.
- An updater that checks this page for new builds and verifies every download by its SHA-256
  checksum.
- License: Apache 2.0, with the notices for everything the installer ships.

BPM and key values are estimates. You need Spotify Premium, your own free Spotify developer app
and your own free FreqBlog key; setup walks you through it.
