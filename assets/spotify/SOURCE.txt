Use is governed by the Spotify Developer Terms and the Design & Branding
Guidelines: white monochrome on dark backgrounds (green only on pure black or
white), full logo at least 70 px wide, icon at least 21 px, exclusion zone of half
the icon's height, no recolouring, rotating, stretching or other changes, and only
to attribute Spotify content. Never on action buttons, never paired with another
brand. The program only scales these images down.
